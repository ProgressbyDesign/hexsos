A Smarter Way to Share Health Knowledge

HExSOS is an innovative community health exchange platform where patients and professionals connect, share lived experiences, explore validated information in a clear, consolidated format, and access personalised support from verified health professionals — all within dynamic, live health topics.

We exist to remove confusion, reduce isolation, and make trustworthy health knowledge easier to find, understand, and act upon.

Our Vision

To empower our community to build a living repository of practical, objective health knowledge — and inspire meaningful collective action on the health issues that matter most.

The HExSOS Story

HExSOS was founded by Kevin Bamrah in response to the limitations of today’s fragmented health information landscape — and the belief that no one should feel helpless in their pain or uncertainty.

Drawing from his own health journey, Kevin recognised a simple but powerful truth:

Someone, somewhere may have information that could help another in need.

From that belief, the Live Topic Models concept was born. HExSOS was created to harness the collective intelligence, lived experience, and professional expertise of community — transforming shared knowledge into meaningful, real-world impact.

How HExSOS Is Structured

The Live Topic Models concept powers three dedicated channels:

search

Mystery Symptoms

Designed for individuals struggling to obtain a diagnosis.

  • Post anonymously (“Hidden from Public”)
  • Community members share suggestions based on lived experience
  • Topics begin as Unsolved
  • Topic creators can mark helpful suggestions and move the topic to Solved
This channel creates hope, direction, and clarity during uncertain health journeys.

How We Ensure Trust & Safety

Reliable information matters. That’s why HExSOS includes structured moderation systems:

Admin Review

New topics begin as Pending Approval and are reviewed for relevance, formatting, accuracy and duplication. Most topics are approved within 24 hours.

Community Flagging

Live topics are monitored through a structured community flagging system reviewed by administrators when thresholds are reached.

Verified Health Professionals

All professionals providing personalised support undergo a thorough credential verification process before offering support in topics within their area of competence.

Trust is built into the system — not left to chance.
